# Workshop aero design notes

The workshop parts are original procedural geometry fitted to the normalized
models, not copies of product meshes, brands or images. The sources below were
used as visual/functional design references; the result is an interpretation,
not an exact replica. The attachment measurements are in `src/body-kits.ts`.

- **Golf Mk4:** R32-style hatch-mounted roof extension, front chin and matching
  skirts, with the twin exhaust grouped on one side. [R32-style kit](https://www.vividracing.com/complete-r32-style-body-kit-volkswagen-golf-0105-p-12944.html), [factory-style roof spoiler](https://vagparts.co.nz/vw-volkswagen-golf-mk4-r32-gti-edition-25-1j-rear-roof-spoiler-1j0827939a-oe-genuine-used-super-rare/).
- **Mercedes W222:** a low front aero lip, sill extensions, rear diffuser and
  deck-mounted spoiler rather than a hatch-style roof wing. [Prior Design W222](https://prior-design.com/tuning/mercedes/s-class/w222-pd800s-aerodynamic-kit), [MEC Design W222](https://mecdesign.de/w222-s-class/w222-s-class-bodykit/).
- **Audi RS6 C8 Avant:** front splitter, side skirts, diffuser and a roof-edge
  spoiler fitted to the existing tailgate roof panel, not a freestanding wing.
  [AUTOID design process](https://autoid.co/blogs/the-mod-blog/how-we-design-a-body-kit-for-the-audi-c8-rs6), [roof extension mounting](https://automotivepassion.net/product/audi-rs6-c8-dry-carbon-rear-roof-spoiler-ap-design/).
- **Mustang:** chin, rocker extensions, diffuser and a deck-mounted wing.
  [1969 Mach 1 front air dam](https://calponycars.com/products/1969-mach-1-mustang-front-spoiler-air-dam-black), [classic rear spoiler examples](https://mustangattitude.com/cgi-bin/specialmustang.cgi?year=1969&model=Mach-1&name=Mustang+Mach+1). The game mesh is a classic fastback, so these references suit it better than modern S550 parts.
- **Ferrari 458:** sculpted splitter and canards, side blades, multi-fin diffuser,
  and circuit-style wing; triple center exhaust on the aggressive option.
  [Prior Design 458](https://prior-design.com/tuning/ferrari/f458/italia-pd458-aerodynamic-kit/), [LB Silhouette GT](https://bulletproofautomotive.com/product/liberty-walk-lb-silhouette-gt-widebody-kit-for-ferrari-458-italia/).
- **Audi R8 Type 42:** front and side diffusers, rear diffuser and GT wing.
  [Liberty Walk R8](https://libertywalk.shop/products/r8/).
- **Dodge Challenger:** broad splitter, rocker extensions, rear diffuser and
  deck wing with more squared muscle-car proportions. [SCL Performance](https://scl-performance.com/en/models/dodge-challenger-tuning-bodykit-mad-max), [ZL1 Addons](https://zl1addons.com/products/challenger-body-kit).

These links do not grant asset or trademark licenses. No assets from them are
included in the game. See the third-party notices for the source models.

## Fitting tools

The optional `tools/inspect_mesh_components.py` uses Blender's `bpy` glTF
importer to inspect connected pieces inside shared material meshes. This was
used to identify the Mustang's original wing and the 458's exhaust components.
The game-space, two-sided rocker surface profiles in `src/sill-profiles.json`
can be regenerated by running `tools/measure_sill_profiles.mjs` against Vite
into a temporary file and then copying it into place (do not truncate the file
while Vite is loading it);
its ranges leave clearance to the front and rear tires. These are measurements
of the existing game assets, not external meshes. `scripts/body-fit-smoke.mjs`
checks the replacement, exhaust origins and wheel clearances.

## Workshop variants and effects

The Touring and Sport settings are additional original procedural profiles,
not imported or copied third-party tuning meshes. They vary dimensions and,
where appropriate, wing supports, sill blades, fins or outlet arrangements;
they are variations on the fitted base designs rather than standalone scanned
body kits. The Golf lip and hatch edge, and RS6 front lip, use measurements
from the in-game bodies. The Ferrari aftermarket exhaust retains the original
three outlet positions rather than introducing a second set of holes.

Afterfire uses a custom animated noise shader and generated geometry in
`src/body-kits.ts`; it does not include an external flame image or model.
These statements do not change the separate license requirements of the car
models themselves, which are described in the third-party notices.
