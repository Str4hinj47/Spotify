        How to play

        • Drive: arrows or WASD to steer and accelerate. Hold down or S to brake, then reverse. Space is handbrake.
        • Circuit: race three laps against five AI rivals. Brake and steer for the bends; finish for pass XP and gems.
        • Highway: overtake traffic safely for points. Close passes and combos score more.
        • Signals: Q / E. Signal your passing side for a bonus.
        • Speed: earn points every half-second above 200 km/h.
        • Camera & pause: C cycles cameras, hold R or the REAR button to look behind, V opens camera settings, P pauses.
        • Rewards: earn coins and credits from runs, and gems from quests.
