How to play

  • Drive it yourself: hold ←/→ or A/D to steer, ↑/W for throttle, ↓/S to brake, Space for handbrake. The road bends and crests — follow it.
  • Graphics: choose scenery detail and render resolution in the top bar or pause menu to balance quality and performance.
  • Sound: open Sound settings in the top bar to control score sounds and indicator clicks separately. The speaker button mutes everything.
  • Indicators: press Q for left or E for right; press again to cancel. Finish an overtake with the signal matching your passing side for +50 points (once per car); the signal cancels automatically. Brake lights brighten when braking.
  • Two-way road: your lane is the right one. The left lane carries oncoming traffic. Pull out to overtake, then get back in before you meet a car head-on.
  • Overtake slower cars in your lane for points. Close passes earn a near-miss bonus; dodging oncoming traffic up close earns extra.
  • Chain clean passes to build a combo multiplier, up to ×5.
  • Modes: Endless is survival; Rush and Near Miss are timed score attacks; Time Trial ends at 3 km. Daily and weekly local objectives rotate at UTC boundaries. Traffic and conditions vary. Records stay on this device and are not verified.
  • Contracts: most runs have three sequential objectives. Daily and weekly runs have one rotating objective and end when it is completed.
  • Traffic: cars follow slower leaders and brake instead of stacking. The hazard warning shows the nearest closing threat.
  • Hit a car or the barrier and the run ends. In Rush, the clock can also end the run. Medals are awarded by score.
  • The cars you didn't pick become traffic. Press C to cycle camera presets (close, chase, far, hood, bumper) or V for camera settings. In the garage, drag to orbit and scroll to zoom.
  • Body workshop: choose car-fitted bumper add-ons, skirts, spoilers and exhaust tips, and pick an underglow color. The choices are cosmetic and saved per car.
  • Paint: in the garage, pick a swatch or a custom color for the selected car. Each car remembers its own paint, and traffic uses that color too. Factory resets it.
  • World: the road crosses meadow, forest, alpine, desert and outskirts biomes with long cross-fades. A day–night cycle changes the sky, stars, headlights and street lamps. Use the world panel (or B) to set time, cycle speed, or force clear/rain/snow. Rain and snow reduce grip.
