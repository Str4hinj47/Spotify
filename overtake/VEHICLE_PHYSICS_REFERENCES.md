# Vehicle physics audit

The game is an arcade driving model with SI-like units (metres, seconds and m/s), not a validated simulation or a claim about the real vehicles represented by its fictional cars. No mass, tire dyno data, engine map, drivetrain layout or aerodynamic dimensions were supplied for those models; inventing them would make the results look more accurate than they are.

## Model and research

- The contact-patch force envelope is approximated by a friction circle: `sqrt(ax_tire² + ay_tire²) <= μg` on level ground. Lateral demand reduces the longitudinal tire budget; propulsion and braking hardware can also demand less than that budget. Grip, tire upgrades and weather are combined once in `vehicleParameters`. A tuned `.85` factor reserves some lateral grip for stability; load transfer and handbrake modifiers remain simplified. [Kamm/friction circle and combined braking/cornering](https://x-engineer.org/braking-cornering-vehicle-skid/).
- Braking capacity is capped by tire grip; increasing brake power past the traction limit does not create extra stopping force. Brake tuning has an arcade hardware multiplier, and the model retains engine braking, so the textbook `v²/(2μg)` is a limiting reference rather than an exact in-game stopping-distance prediction. [Braking distance and friction](https://www.onlycalculators.com/other/dynamics/stopping-distance-calculator/).
- Road load is approximated as a constant rolling component plus a quadratic speed term, with additional off-throttle engine braking. Without measured vehicle mass, drag coefficient and frontal area, the existing per-car acceleration-equivalent coefficients remain game tuning rather than calculated factory values. [ICCT: light-duty vehicle driving resistances](https://theicct.org/sites/default/files/publications/ICCT_LDV-Driving-Resistances-EU_121516.pdf).
- Collision contacts use an equal-mass, low-restitution impulse with a limited tangential friction impulse. They are deliberately simplified and preserve the player's commanded heading rather than simulate body deformation, unequal mass or off-centre angular impulse. [Rigid-body contact impulse overview](https://interactivecomputergraphics.github.io/physics-simulation/examples/rigid_body_collision.html).
- Garage sprint and stopping distances are derived by stepping the same vehicle model. The terminal-speed calculation includes both engine/drag equilibrium and a traction cap.

Automated checks cover the friction envelope, braking saturation and wet grip, displayed performance vs the actual integrator, circuit driving, drift and race balance. Human driving assessment remains necessary. The model does not include individual wheel slip, ABS, gears, suspension, vertical load transfer, tire temperature, damage, or aerodynamic downforce.
